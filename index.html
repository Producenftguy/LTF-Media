How it works

From first call to running on its own

Most stores are fully set up within two weeks. After that, it runs without taking your time.

  1. Day 1

    A free 20-minute call

    Tell us what's eating your time. Is it the OTC paperwork piling up, the Instagram nobody posts to, or the Google listing with last year's hours? We listen first and sell nothing on this call.

  2. Week 1

    We walk your store

    We come in person and look at it the way your customers do: the aisles, the register, which benefit cards you accept, your Google listing, your reviews and your social pages. Most owners learn at least one thing they didn't know.

  3. Week 2

    Your one-page plan

    A single page that shows what's costing you customers, what we'll fix first, the dates, and one monthly price. You can say yes to all of it or just one piece.

  4. Every day after

    We run it, you see it

    We post, file, fix and follow up. Daily OTC updates go to your business email, and once a month you get a short report: what we did, what changed, and what's next.

Questions

What owners usually ask

Can you guarantee my store gets approved for OTC or EBT?

No one honestly can. The card programs and USDA make that decision. We make sure the forms are complete, the documents are right and nothing is late, which is where most renewals go wrong.

How much of my time does this take?

About an hour to get started, then a short check-in each month. We come to the store when we need photos or video.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. For OTC and EBT setup, you pay a one-time introduction fee to get started, and nothing more while we wait for approval. After that, we take six months to show our work and results, then it's month to month.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Family-run food businesses: supermarkets, grocers, bodegas, fish markets and meat markets. Most of our experience is in independent, family-run stores.

Which areas do you cover?

All five boroughs and Long Island.
